About RoadNextGen

Turning citizen reports into
faster, smarter repairs

A civic-tech platform connecting citizens, municipality administrators, and road crews — powered by AI to keep roads safe.

AI Detection Engine

YOLOv11-powered model classifies potholes, cracks, and waterlogging from citizen photos with 94%+ accuracy in real time.

Live Hazard Map

Every report is pinned on a live interactive Leaflet map, giving full visibility into nearby hazards and active repair zones.

Full Repair Lifecycle

From citizen report to crew assignment to marked-complete — every stakeholder stays informed at each stage automatically.

Multi-Role Access

Separate portals for citizens (OTP/Google), municipality admins, and maintenance crew heads with role-based dashboards.

Cloudinary Uploads

Repair photos uploaded securely via Cloudinary — before and after images build accountability and verify completion.

Smart Notifications

Citizens receive email updates when their report is accepted, assigned to crew, and resolved.

How It Works

Hazard to fix in 4 steps

RoadNextGen turns citizen observations into actionable repair tasks — fast, transparent, and fully tracked.

01

Citizen Reports

Citizen submits a photo. AI instantly classifies damage type, severity, and location — pinned on the live map.

02

Admin Reviews

Municipality admin verifies the report, sets priority, and assigns a maintenance crew to the repair task.

03

Crew Dispatched

Crew navigates to the site using integrated maps, logs repair progress, and uploads before/after photos.

04

Resolved & Verified

Citizen notified via email. Hazard automatically removed from the live map. Repair record archived.
